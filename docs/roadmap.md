Serial integration, Raspberry Pi/STM32 workflows, live telemetry, voice, mobile camera workflow, robotics debugging, HDL/FPGA tools.

## V4

Collaborative workspaces, hardware-in-the-loop testing, richer MCP ecosystem, community plugins and agents.
